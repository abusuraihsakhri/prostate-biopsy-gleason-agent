Clinical scope and limitations

  • Implements the nine standard combinations of assigned biopsy Gleason patterns 3, 4, and 5 and their ISUP Grade Groups 1–5.
  • The designation of architectural patterns and selection of the secondary or highest-grade component must be made according to contemporary biopsy reporting practice.
  • Pattern 4 percentage, cribriform carcinoma, IDC-P, and specimen-level or case-level grading are not assessed here.
  • No diagnostic conclusions, risk groups, management recommendations, or clinical validation are provided.

References: EAU classification and grading; CAP prostate biopsy protocols.

Operates in-browser with JavaScript, without an API, uploads, analytics, cookies, or browser storage. For education and calculation verification; not validated for autonomous clinical use.
